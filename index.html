SABLE RIFT

Combat & trading in a system on the edge of a black hole

Mouse steer (F toggles) · L / R click lasers / missile
Arrows pitch & yaw (Up = nose down, I inverts) · Q / E roll
W / S or + / − throttle · Backspace full stop · hold Tab afterburner
Space lasers · Enter missile · X decoy
T cycle targets · R nearest hostile
N cycle nav points · M nav map · A autopilot
D request docking · V external view · U music · K mute · P / Esc / H pause & help
Gamepad: left stick pitch/yaw · right stick roll · RT fire · LT afterburner · A missile · B decoy · X/Y target · RB/LB throttle · D-pad autopilot/dock/nav/map · Start pause · Back view

Best with keyboard + mouse. Missiles need a lock: keep a hostile in your sights.
