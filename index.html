SABLE RIFT

Combat & trading in a system on the edge of a black hole

Mouse steer (F toggles) · L / R click lasers / missile · hold Alt to free the cursor and click a ship to target it
Arrows pitch & yaw (Up = nose down, I inverts) · Q / E roll
W / S or + / − thrust · Backspace cut thrust · hold Tab afterburner · hold C brake · hold Z reverse thrust
Y flight assist (cancels drift) · hold B match target velocity · fly through cargo pods to scoop them
Space lasers · Enter missile · G missile type · X decoy
T cycle targets · R nearest hostile
N cycle nav points · M nav map · A autopilot · J jump (at a jump gate)
D request docking · V external view · F2 chase cam · U music · K mute · P / Esc / H pause & help
Gamepad: left stick pitch/yaw · right stick roll · RT fire · LT afterburner · A missile · B decoy · X/Y target · RB/LB thrust · R3 brake · L3 missile type · D-pad autopilot/dock-or-jump/nav/map · Start pause · Back view

Newtonian flight: you keep drifting until you thrust the other way. Use the prograde/retrograde markers. Touch screens get on-screen controls.
